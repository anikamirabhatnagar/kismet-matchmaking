Meet Mala - The Heart and Soul Behind Kismet

Mala is a natural connector—intuitive, warm, and deeply people-focused.

While Mala's professional background includes design, technology marketing, and business development, her true passion lies in connecting like-minded individuals. She seamlessly blends emotional intelligence with strategic vision, transforming ideas into meaningful connections and lasting impact.

For over 30 years, Mala has built trusted, enduring networks within the South Asian community across the U.S., guided by her authentic, honest, and caring nature. Her sincerity and intuition have helped countless people form meaningful connections.

Since launching Kismet in 2025, she has already created over 20 qualified, intentional matches—each one a testament to her dedication and heart-led approach to matchmaking.

Why Matchmaking Matters

Dating is a vital part of discovering compatibility and building a meaningful relationship. Mala encourages intentional dating and follows the 3-6-9 Month Rule—a simple framework to understand the natural progression of a relationship.

Dating apps can help you meet people, but they lack accountability, structure, and depth. A skilled matchmaker understands you, curates introductions with care, and provides guidance to nurture lasting relationships.
